01Who we are

Atlasio builds the infrastructure to capture, structure and deliver interactive 3D twins of physical and virtual spaces.

We help cities and companies turn scans, sensors and records into one navigable space, where every building, room or asset carries its own data.

Context is what makes data useful. Atlasio pairs each scene with guided stories and an AI layer that explains what you are looking at.
